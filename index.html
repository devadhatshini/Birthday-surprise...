Happy Birthday My Dear Maama! ❤️🎂

Unakku first-ah birthday wish panradhu naan dhaan irukkanum nu aasai patten. ❤️

Enga appavukku aduthapadiya en life-la naan romba nambura oru person nee dhaan. Enakku nee oru husband mattum illa, enna endha vishayama irundhaalum comfortable-ah, free-ah endha hesitation-um illaama pesura oru person. Unkitta en manasula irukkuradha ellame open-ah share panna mudiyum. Andha alavukku nee enakku special. 💖

Nee indha birthday-ku naan advance wishes pannuvannu romba expect panniruppa. Aana naan advance-ah wish panna mudiyala. Unakku special-ah indha naal-la wish pannanum nu dhaan wait pannen. ❤️

Nee un life-la neraya achieve pannanum. Unakku aasai padra ellame kidaikkanum. Andha aasai list-la ennaiyum serthu! 🥰

Nee neraya times enkitte "Love You" sollirukka. But naan sila times sollama irundhirukken. Aana un mela irukkura anbu eppovum maaradhu.

You are my first love, and I want to be your last love too. En life-la nee evlo special-nu words-la solla mudiyadhu. Enakku eppovum un first love-ah irukkanum; unakku last love-um naan dhaan irukkanum. ❤️

Naan unna neraya thadava thittirukken. "Unkitta idhu illa, adhu illa… ippadi iru, appadi iru" nu neraya sollirukken. Aana nee adhukellam kovapadama, edhu sonnaalum "Sari, sari" nu amaidhiya accept pannippa. Adhukku naan unakku romba thankful. ❤️

Naan eppodhaavadhu theriyama unna hurt aagura maari pesirundhaa, adha manasula vachikkadha. Enna mannichidu. Unkitta edhu irundhaalum, illainaalum, naan eppovum un pakkam dhaan iruppen. Unakku support-ah, un kooda, un happiness-layum kashtathulayum iruppen. Idhu en promise. 🫂

Love You So Much! ❤️

Eppovum ippadiye en kooda irukkanum. Happy Birthday Maama! ❤️🫂
